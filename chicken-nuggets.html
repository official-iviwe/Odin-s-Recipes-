Chicken Nuggets Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts

  • 1 cup flour

  • 1 egg

  • 1 cup breadcrumbs

  • Salt and pepper

  • Cooking oil

Method


  1. Cut chicken into small pieces.

  2. Season with salt and pepper.

  3. Coat each piece in flour.

  4. Dip into beaten egg.

  5. Cover with breadcrumbs.

  6. Fry in hot oil until golden and cooked through.

  7. Drain on paper towels and serve with tomato sauce.
