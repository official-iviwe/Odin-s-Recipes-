Beef Stew Recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef

  • 1 onion

  • 2 potatoes

  • 2 carrots

  • 2 tomatoes

  • 1 cup water

  • salt and curry powder

Method


  1. Fry the onion in a pot.

  2. Add beef and cook until brown.

  3. Add tomatoes, salt and curry powder.

  4. Add water and simmer until the beef is tender.

  5. Add potatoes and carrots.

  6. Serve hot with rice or pap.
